Concrete 5-Step Classification Pipeline

  • Step 1: OEM & Part # Domain Gating: Analyzes Manufacturer Name and Part # to lock allowable industrial sectors (e.g. Apparel Segment 53, Medical Segment 42, Lab Equipment Segment 41, Tools Segment 27).
  • Step 2: Semantic Head Noun Extraction: Strips transient attributes (colors, sizes, packaging counts) using strict word boundaries to identify the true functional object (e.g. T-shirt, Catheter, Syringe, Socket).
  • Step 3: Supplier & SKU Cross-Check: Uses distributor context and catalog part numbers for secondary verification when OEM data is ambiguous.
  • Step 4: Hierarchical Taxonomy Mapping: Evaluates across Segment (2-digit), Family (4-digit), Class (6-digit), and Commodity (8-digit).
  • Step 5: Strict Doubt & Rollup Policy:
    • No Doubt — Exact match, assigned to Commodity (8-digit)
    • Some Doubt — Subtype/variant ambiguity, rolled up to Class (6-digit) or Family (4-digit)
    • Huge Doubt — Vague or conflicting signals, rolled up to Segment (2-digit)

What is UNSPSC?

The United Nations Standard Products and Services Code (UNSPSC) is a hierarchical taxonomy for classifying products and services. It has 4 levels:

  1. Segment (2 digits) — Broadest category (e.g., "Defense and Law Enforcement...")
  2. Family (4 digits) — Major grouping (e.g., "Personal safety and protection")
  3. Class (6 digits) — Sub-grouping (e.g., "Safety apparel")
  4. Commodity (8 digits) — Specific product (e.g., "Protective gloves")
